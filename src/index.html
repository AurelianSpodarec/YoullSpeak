We have organized group talks especially for people who wants to learn new things from others and help to develop speaking skills of foreign languages. That’s why we don’t have groups more than for 4 persons. For people who will join overfull groups we suggest to stay muted, this option will be useful for beginners learners.

In our Community group you can easily find interesting people and start to talk with them without any delay, all what you need to do just join online group and start to practice English, Spanish, French or any other language.

If you are free right now you will speak!

In our community you can be in touch with everyone you want, you can easily find friends or write a review to your partner from last talk and suggest to speak again or even agree a meeating in real life! You also can give a stars to advanced speakers, this option will help you to find a good speaker with equal skills in foreign language for future talks.

We made this service because we value of your time and your friendship with members of You’ll Speak.
